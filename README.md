<section id="anatomy">
    <h2>What’s Inside a Vape?</h2>
    <div class="card">
        <p>
            Disposable and reusable e-cigarettes are made of multiple components that combine
            plastics, metals, electronics, and chemical liquids. These parts make recycling difficult
            and contribute to environmental harm when improperly discarded.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="stats">
        <div class="stat">
            <h3>Plastic</h3>
            <p>
                Outer casing and mouthpiece. Breaks down into microplastics in the environment.
            </p>
        </div>

        <div class="stat">
            <h3>Lithium Battery</h3>
            <p>
                Powers the device. Can cause fires and leaks toxic chemicals if damaged.
            </p>
        </div>

        <div class="stat">
            <h3>Heating Coil</h3>
            <p>
                Metal coil (often nickel or chromium) that heats the liquid into aerosol.
            </p>
        </div>

        <div class="stat">
            <h3>E-Liquid</h3>
            <p>
                Contains nicotine, flavorings, propylene glycol, and vegetable glycerin.
                Can leave toxic residue when discarded.
            </p>
        </div>
    </div>

<section id="air">
    <h2>Environmental Impact During Use</h2>

<img src="images/vape-aerosol.jpg"
     alt="Person exhaling vape aerosol"
     class="section-image">

<p class="image-caption">
    Vape aerosols release fine particles and chemicals into indoor environments.
</p>

    <div class="card">
        <h3>Indoor Air Pollution</h3>
        <p>
            Although e-cigarettes do not burn tobacco, they release aerosols containing nicotine,
            ultrafine particles, volatile organic compounds (VOCs), aldehydes, heavy metals, and
            other potentially hazardous substances.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
        <h3>Secondhand and Thirdhand Exposure</h3>
        <p>
            More than 70% of inhaled vape aerosol is eventually exhaled into the surrounding
            environment. These emissions can affect indoor air quality and expose bystanders,
            including children and non-users, to airborne pollutants.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
        <h3>Environmental Contaminants</h3>
        <ul>
            <li>Nicotine residues</li>
            <li>Lead, nickel, chromium, and copper</li>
            <li>Fine particulate matter (PM2.5)</li>
            <li>Volatile organic compounds (VOCs)</li>
            <li>Persistent PFAS chemicals</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
</section>

<section id="waste">
    <h2>The Vape Waste Problem</h2>
    <img src="https://www.aces.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/shutterstock_2269905681-scaled.jpg"
            alt="Vape litter"
            class="section-image"
            style="width:75%;">  
    <div class="card">
        <h3>Disposable Vapes = Electronic Waste</h3>
        <p>
            Each disposable vape contains plastic, circuitry, lithium-ion batteries,
            metals, and leftover e-liquid. Because these materials are combined into
            a small device, recycling is difficult and often impossible.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
        <h3>Environmental Risks</h3>
        <ul>
            <li>Battery fires in garbage trucks and landfills</li>
            <li>Heavy-metal contamination of soil and water</li>
            <li>Microplastic pollution from plastic casings</li>
            <li>Nicotine leaching into ecosystems</li>
            <li>Loss of valuable materials such as lithium</li>
        </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
        <h3>Learn More</h3>

<section id="solutions">
    <h2>Potential Solutions</h2>

    <div class="card">
        <ul>
            <li>Ban or reduce single-use disposable vape products.</li>
            <li>Require manufacturers to fund recycling programs.</li>
            <li>Create convenient vape take-back locations.</li>
            <li>Design reusable and repairable vaping devices.</li>
            <li>Educate consumers about proper disposal methods.</li>
            <li>Expand e-waste collection infrastructure.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
        <h3>Key Takeaway</h3>
        <p>
            Vaping is often discussed as a health issue, but it is also an environmental issue.
            The rapid growth of disposable vaping devices has created a new stream of electronic
            waste that combines plastic pollution, toxic chemicals, and lithium battery disposal
            challenges. Addressing vape waste requires changes in product design, regulation,
            and consumer behavior.
        </p>
    </div>
